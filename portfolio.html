Healthcare & Hospital

Apollo Crest Specialty Hospital Portal

Core Metric +340% Appointments
Edge Speed 0.42s LCP

The Challenge:

Apollo Crest had a legacy slow website that suffered from 6-second load times. Over 40% of prospective patients dropped off before completing appointment booking. Phone lines were jammed with lab report inquiries.

BananaTech Solution:

We migrated their platform to a Jamstack architecture hosted globally on Cloudflare Pages. Integrated a real-time doctor roster with automated WhatsApp appointment tokens and a secure PIN-authenticated lab report repository.

Hospitality & Dining Tech

SpiceCraft Rooftop & Lounge

Commission Saved ₹1.8L / month
Dine-in Order Cycle 42 Seconds

The Challenge:

Paying 28% commissions to Swiggy & Zomato for online deliveries severely cut into restaurant profit margins. Weekend dine-in customers waited 15+ minutes just for physical menus during peak hours.

BananaTech Solution:

Engineered QR contactless table menus that diners scan to browse high-definition dish photos and place orders directly to kitchen thermal printers. Built a direct delivery checkout with instant UPI payment.

Cross-Platform Mobile

PulseHealth Telemedicine iOS & Android

App Store Rating 4.8 / 5.0 ★
Time to Market 38 Days

The Challenge:

The client needed a frictionless mobile app allowing patients in tier-2 cities with weak cellular connectivity to perform video consultations and sync chronic illness vitals without crashes.

BananaTech Solution:

Created a lightweight Flutter application backed by Cloudflare Workers and Supabase Realtime. Integrated adaptive WebRTC video that automatically scales video resolution to preserve clear voice on 3G/4G connections.
