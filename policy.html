Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: October 2, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to BananaTech (bananatech.in). We design and engineer web platforms, mobile applications, software products, and executive digital profiles.

We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our clients, website visitors, and partners. This policy outlines how we collect, handle, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you directly and voluntarily provide to us when inquiring about our services:

  • Contact Information: Your name, business email address, phone number, and WhatsApp contact details.
  • Project Specifications: Details, project goals, timeline requirements, and technical scope submitted via our contact forms.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Minimal, anonymized device diagnostics (such as browser type and latency) used strictly to verify network performance and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used solely to:

  • Review project briefs and deliver technical proposals, roadmaps, and estimates.
  • Communicate directly regarding scoping calls and project deliverables.
  • Maintain operational and technical support throughout your project lifecycle.

✓ We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or business data to any third party.

4. Client Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

BananaTech operates on a strict 100% Client Ownership model:

  • Upon project completion and settlement of contracted deliverables, all custom source code, design assets, and repositories belong entirely to you.
  • We do not enforce proprietary vendor lock-in or licensing fees on custom deliverables.
  • All proprietary business logic, client documents, and project briefs are treated under strict confidentiality (NDA standards).

5. Security & Data Protection

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) across all web platforms. Communication and project repositories are protected using multi-factor authenticated environments to safeguard your code and specifications against unauthorized access.
